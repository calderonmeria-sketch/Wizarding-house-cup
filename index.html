    ⚠️ Protect Your House Points!

    Follow the rules and help your house win the House Cup!

    🪄 Discipline

    1. Stay at your desk and avoid walking around the classroom without permission.
    2. Focus on the class and avoid playing or doing something unrelated to the activity.
    3. Keep the classroom quiet and avoid shouting or talking too loudly.

    🤝 Respect

    1. Listen carefully when the teacher or classmates are speaking.
    2. Do not interrupt others when they are speaking.
    3. Raise your hand when you want to speak.
    4. Respect other people's turn to speak and avoid speaking over someone else.
    ⚠️ Breaking these rules may cause your house to lose points!

    💾 Data Management

    Data is saved automatically to this browser's local storage. This project is structured so it can later connect to Firebase or Supabase (see script.js — DataStore).

    💡 If you're running this file locally (downloaded, opened by double-clicking it) and notice your data disappears when you reopen it, some browsers restrict storage for local files. Use 📊 Export Data right before closing, and 📂 Import Data the next time you open the file, to carry your progress over reliably.
